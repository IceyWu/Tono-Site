Welcome to LiuBai. If you have a question or run into trouble, we hope this page helps.

## Using LiuBai

- **Writing notes**: open LiuBai and start recording your ideas and thoughts. The editor stays out of the way so you can focus on the words.
- **Catching ideas**: jot down a passing thought anywhere, any time, so no idea slips away.
- **Organizing**: use lists and categories to keep your notes in order.

## FAQ

**Does LiuBai sync to the cloud?**

Not currently. All LiuBai data is stored locally on your device and is never uploaded to the cloud. We recommend backing up your device regularly to protect your data.

**Does LiuBai collect my personal information?**

No. LiuBai collects no personally identifiable information and does not track your usage. See our [Privacy Policy](/en/liubai/privacy) for details.

**Does LiuBai have ads or in-app purchases?**

LiuBai contains no third-party advertising.

**Which system versions are supported?**

LiuBai requires iOS/iPadOS 17.0 or later.

**How do I back up my notes?**

Back up your device with iCloud or iTunes/Finder — LiuBai's data is included in the system backup.
