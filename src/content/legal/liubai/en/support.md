Welcome to LiuBai. If you have a question or run into trouble, we hope this page helps.

## Using LiuBai

- **Writing notes**: open LiuBai to record ideas and thoughts; the editor saves your work automatically.
- **More ways to remember**: write a note, answer the daily prompt, add a photo, or record a voice clip.
- **Organizing**: use Notes, Calendar, Favorites, and Trash to manage your entries.
- **Private sync**: when iCloud is enabled, words, photos, and recordings sync across your devices through your private CloudKit database.

## FAQ

**Can I use LiuBai without iCloud?**

Yes. LiuBai is local-first. You can create, edit, and view content on the current device when you are not signed in to an Apple Account, iCloud is disabled, or you are temporarily offline.

**What does LiuBai sync?**

When the device is signed in to an Apple Account with iCloud enabled, note titles, text, tags, favorite and trash status, photos, and recordings sync through Apple CloudKit to your private iCloud database.

**Why has my latest content not appeared on another device?**

Make sure both devices use the same Apple Account, have iCloud enabled, have available iCloud storage, and are connected to the network. Sync can be delayed by network conditions or Apple service status; keep LiuBai open briefly and check again.

**Does LiuBai collect my personal information?**

LiuBai requires no separate app account, operates no user-content server, and contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. iCloud sync is handled by Apple CloudKit. See our [Privacy Policy](/en/liubai/privacy) for details.

**Does LiuBai have ads or in-app purchases?**

LiuBai contains no third-party advertising and currently offers no in-app purchases.

**Which system versions are supported?**

LiuBai requires iOS/iPadOS 17.0 or later.

**How can I protect my notes?**

With iCloud enabled, CloudKit syncs content across your own devices. You can also back up the device with iCloud or Finder for additional protection. CloudKit sync and device backup are separate mechanisms; the App currently provides no independent export feature.

**What happens if I delete LiuBai?**

Deleting the App removes its local copy from the current device. Content already synced may remain in your iCloud and may become available on other supported devices or after reinstallation. You can manage related data in system iCloud settings.
