## 1. Introduction

LiuBai ("we") takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Local-first

LiuBai's core features (writing notes, capturing and organizing ideas) run entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

### 2.2 What we do not collect

- No personally identifiable information
- No note content
- No usage tracking
- No Required Reason APIs

## 3. Use of Information

The app contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not collect or transmit diagnostic information.

## 4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or share any of your information with third parties.

## 5. Data Security

- Your notes are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to any server
- We do not store any of your personal data

## 6. Your Rights

At any time you can:

- Manage the app's permissions in your system settings
- Delete the app to remove all local data

## 7. Minors

The app is suitable for users of all ages (rated 4+). If you are a minor, please use it with the guidance of a guardian.

## 8. Policy Updates

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page.
