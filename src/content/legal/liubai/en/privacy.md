## 1. Introduction

LiuBai (the “App”) is a local-first writing app with private iCloud sync. This policy explains how the App handles your content and related information.

## 2. How Data Is Handled

### 2.1 Local-first

You can continue creating, editing, and managing notes on the current device when you are not signed in to an Apple Account, iCloud is disabled, or sync is temporarily unavailable.

### 2.2 Private iCloud sync

When the device is signed in to an Apple Account with iCloud enabled, note titles, text, tags, favorite and trash status, and photos or recordings you add are stored through Apple CloudKit in your private iCloud database and synced across supported devices using the same Apple Account.

Sync depends on iCloud settings, available storage, network conditions, and Apple service status. It may be delayed or temporarily unavailable.

### 2.3 What the developer does not collect

- No separate app account is required
- Your notes, photos, and recordings are not uploaded to a content server operated by the developer
- User content is not used for advertising, analytics, sale, or cross-app tracking
- The App contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs

## 3. Use of Data

User content is used only to record, organize, and display entries in the App and to sync them through CloudKit across your own devices. The developer does not use this content for profiling, marketing, or advertising.

## 4. Apple System Services

Private iCloud sync is provided by Apple CloudKit. Apple Account authentication and CloudKit data transfer and storage are handled by Apple system services and are subject to Apple’s applicable terms and privacy policies.

## 5. Data Management and Deletion

At any time, you can:

- Manage LiuBai’s photo, microphone, notification, and iCloud permissions in system settings
- Delete entries in the App; deletions are reflected in the same private iCloud database when sync is available
- Manage related cloud data through system iCloud settings

Deleting the App removes its local copy from the current device. Content already synced may remain in your iCloud and may become available on other supported devices or after reinstallation. The developer cannot sign in to your Apple Account, manage iCloud settings, or restore content on your behalf.

## 6. Data Security

LiuBai does not operate an independent user-content server. Protect your device passcode and Apple Account, and keep any additional backups appropriate for important content. CloudKit sync keeps content consistent across devices; it is not a separate long-term archive or exported copy.

## 7. Minors

The App is suitable for users of all ages (rated 4+). If you are a minor, please use it with the guidance of a guardian.
